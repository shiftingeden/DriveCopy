DriveCopy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

DriveCopy is built so that your data never leaves your hands. The short version: we collect nothing, transmit nothing, and see nothing.

What DriveCopy does with your files

DriveCopy copies files between USB drives you connect to your device and backups stored on your device. All copying happens locally, on your iPhone. Your files are never uploaded, analyzed, indexed, or shared. The app has no server and makes no network connections.

What we collect

Nothing. DriveCopy has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We never see your files, file names, or anything about your drives.

Where your backups live

Backups are stored in DriveCopy's own folder on your device, visible in the Files app under On My iPhone → DriveCopy. They stay on your device unless you move them yourself. Deleting the app deletes its backups; deleting a backup inside the app removes only the copy on your device, never the files on your drives. If you use iCloud device backup, your DriveCopy backups may be included in it like any other app data — that is between your device and Apple, and we have no access to it.

Purchases

The optional DriveCopy Pro unlock is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from the transaction.

Children

DriveCopy collects no data from anyone, including children.
